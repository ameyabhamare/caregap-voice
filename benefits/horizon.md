# Horizon ACA Marketplace (Silver EPO): Summary of Benefits 2025

Synthetic document for the CareGap Voice demo. Horizon ACA Marketplace is not a real
health plan, and this is not a real Summary of Benefits.

## 1. About this plan

Horizon ACA Marketplace Silver is an EPO plan sold on the health insurance marketplace for
people under 65 who live in the plan's service area in Massachusetts. You can see any
provider in the network without a referral. Care outside the network is not covered,
except emergency care. Member Services: 1-800-555-0120 (TTY 711), 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.,
Monday to Friday.

## 2. Premium and deductibles

Monthly premium: depends on your age and household income, and on any premium tax credit
you receive. Your premium is shown on your enrollment notice.
Medical deductible: $3,000 per person or $6,000 per family each year.
Prescription drug deductible: $500 per person each year. It applies to Tier 2 and Tier 3
drugs only.

## 3. Maximum out-of-pocket

Maximum out-of-pocket amount for in-network care: $8,500 per person or $17,000 per family
each year. It includes your deductible, copays, coinsurance and prescription drug costs.

## 4. Doctor visits

Primary care visit: $30 copay. The deductible does not apply.
Specialist visit: $70 copay after you meet the deductible. No referral is needed.
Telehealth visit: $10 copay.

## 5. Preventive care

Preventive care visit: $0, once per year. The deductible does not apply.
Recommended screenings and vaccines, including flu shots: $0.

## 6. Eye care

Adult routine eye exams and adult glasses are not covered.
Diabetic eye exam (retinal exam): covered as a specialist visit, $70 copay after you meet
the deductible.
Children under 19: one eye exam per year, $0, and one pair of glasses per year, $0.

## 7. Emergency and urgent care

Emergency room visit: 40% coinsurance after you meet the deductible.
Urgent care center visit: $75 copay.
Ground ambulance: 40% coinsurance after you meet the deductible.

## 8. Hospital and surgery

Inpatient hospital stay: 40% coinsurance after you meet the deductible. Prior
authorization is required.
Outpatient surgery: 40% coinsurance after you meet the deductible.

## 9. Prescription drugs

Cost per 30-day supply at a network pharmacy:
Tier 1 (generic): $15 copay.
Tier 2 (preferred brand): $50 copay after the drug deductible.
Tier 3 (non-preferred): $100 copay after the drug deductible.
Tier 4 (specialty): 50% coinsurance, up to $350 per prescription.
Most cholesterol-lowering statins are on Tier 1.
A 90-day supply by mail order costs 2.5 times the 30-day copay.

## 10. Extra benefits

This plan has no adult dental, hearing aid, gym membership or over-the-counter allowance
benefits.

## 11. What this plan does not cover

Adult dental care, adult routine eye exams and glasses, hearing aids, cosmetic surgery,
acupuncture, long-term custodial care, and care from out-of-network providers except
emergency care.
