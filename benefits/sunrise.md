# Sunrise Medicare Advantage (HMO): Summary of Benefits 2025

Synthetic document for the CareGap Voice demo. Sunrise Medicare Advantage is not a real
health plan, and this is not a real Summary of Benefits.

## 1. About this plan

Sunrise Medicare Advantage is a Medicare Advantage HMO plan for people with Medicare Part A
and Part B who live in the plan's service area in Massachusetts. Members choose a primary
care provider (PCP) from the plan's network. Referrals are required to see specialists.
Care from providers outside the network is not covered, except emergency and urgent care.
Member Services: 1-800-555-0110 (TTY 711), 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., seven days a week.

## 2. Premium and deductibles

Monthly plan premium: $0. You must keep paying your Medicare Part B premium.
Medical deductible: $0.
Prescription drug deductible: $250 per year. It applies to drugs on Tiers 3, 4 and 5 only.

## 3. Maximum out-of-pocket

Maximum out-of-pocket amount for in-network services: $4,900 per year. After you reach it,
the plan pays 100% of covered medical services for the rest of the year. Prescription drug
costs do not count toward this amount.

## 4. Doctor visits

Primary care provider visit: $0 copay.
Specialist visit: $35 copay. A referral from your PCP is required.
Telehealth visit with a network provider: $0 copay.

## 5. Preventive care

Annual wellness visit: $0 copay, once per calendar year.
Flu and pneumonia shots: $0 copay.
Covered cancer screenings, including mammograms and colonoscopies: $0 copay.

## 6. Eye care

Diabetic eye exam (retinal exam): $0 copay, once per calendar year.
Routine eye exam: $0 copay, once per calendar year.
Eyewear allowance: up to $150 every year toward glasses or contact lenses.

## 7. Emergency and urgent care

Emergency room visit: $110 copay. You do not pay this copay if you are admitted to the
hospital within 24 hours.
Urgent care center visit: $40 copay.
Ground ambulance: $275 copay per trip.

## 8. Hospital and surgery

Inpatient hospital stay: $350 copay per day for days 1 to 5, then $0 for the rest of the
stay. Prior authorization is required.
Outpatient surgery: $250 copay.

## 9. Prescription drugs

Cost per 30-day supply at a preferred retail pharmacy:
Tier 1 (preferred generic): $0 copay.
Tier 2 (generic): $10 copay.
Tier 3 (preferred brand): $47 copay.
Tier 4 (non-preferred drug): $100 copay.
Tier 5 (specialty): 33% coinsurance.
Most cholesterol-lowering statins are on Tier 1.
A 90-day supply by mail order costs $0 for Tier 1 and Tier 2 drugs.

## 10. Extra benefits

Dental: two cleanings and exams per year, $0 copay.
Hearing aids: up to $1,000 every year toward hearing aids.
Fitness: gym membership at participating gyms, $0.
Over-the-counter allowance: $50 every three months for health items.
Transportation: 24 one-way trips per year to medical appointments.

## 11. What this plan does not cover

Cosmetic surgery, acupuncture, long-term custodial care in a nursing home, and care from
out-of-network providers except emergency and urgent care.
